# Asset manifest

All photography is **free-to-use stock under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)**, verified on each photo's public Unsplash page (license line, photographer, CDN path) in November 2026. Source of truth: `src/content/images.ts` (43 images). Attribution is shown on the site under **Footer → Photography credits** with Unsplash's referral parameters.

> **These are stand-ins.** AZURE is fictional. The dishes and rooms pictured belong to other restaurants and photographers. A real launch must replace every image with the restaurant's own commissioned photography (see the brief below) — never present stock dishes as a real kitchen's food.

## Verification limits

- Licence, photographer and subject were verified from each photo's Unsplash page **metadata**. The build sandbox could not download images (the network allowlist blocks image CDNs), so **colour, composition and crop were not visually reviewed by the builder**. Review the set in a browser before showing the site to a client — swapping an image is a one-line change in `images.ts`.
- Dish photos were matched to dishes by their Unsplash descriptions (e.g. "oysters on ice with lemon wedges" → _Rock oysters_). Where a description was generic, the dish copy was written to stay accurate to it.
- Images are hot-linked from Unsplash's CDN (as Unsplash's API guidelines require) via a custom `next/image` loader, which requests the exact width per `srcset` entry and lets the CDN negotiate AVIF/WebP. No host-side image optimisation runs, which keeps free-tier quotas untouched.

## Commissioning brief for the real shoot

- **Light**: natural window light by day, warm low practicals by night; no flash, no blue grading on food.
- **Food**: overhead and 30° angles on the house ceramics; 4:5 and 5:4 crops with generous negative space for type.
- **Rooms**: wide architectural frames (16:9 / 21:9) at dusk for heroes; detail frames of materials (stone, oak, glass) in 3:4.
- **People**: the real chef and team at work (hands, pass, fire) rather than posed portraits.
- **Deliverables**: originals ≥ 3000px on the long edge, sRGB, with a written release for anyone pictured.
